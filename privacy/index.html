privacy

what we keep, in plain words. it isn't much. last changed november 2026.

the short version

no accounts. no ads. no trackers. no analytics. we don't sell or share anything about you.

what stays on your own device

the house remembers a few small things in your browser so it doesn't ask twice: that the door opened for you, that you've seen the opening, your mute button, and which potato you dug up in the garden. that never leaves your device. clear your browser's site data and it's gone.

what reaches us

  • the list. if you type your email into "join the list", we keep that email so potato can write to you. nothing else. ask and we'll delete it.
  • a door code. if you type a one-time code at the door, it is checked once and marked used. it isn't tied to your name.
  • email. if you write to us, we have your email, like anyone you write to.
  • the coupon. to redeem a coupon for a potato you give an email, and we send six numbers to it to check it's yours. we keep that email, which potato you got and his spud number, so there's one coupon each and so a dropped connection can give you the same potato back. that's all it's used for. you're not put on the list unless you tick the box.
  • your potato's key. we never have it. it isn't sent to us, we don't store it and we never mail it. if you lose it, we can't get it back for you.
  • your potato. which potato is yours is remembered on your own device. clearing that device's data forgets him.

who else is in the room

  • the pages are served by a web host, which sees what every host sees: your IP address and which page you asked for.
  • the lettering comes from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google for the font.
  • the list and the keys are kept with a Google service that potato's mail room runs on.
  • the trailer plays from YouTube, in its no-cookie mode, only if you open that page.

kids

this is made for adults. we don't knowingly collect anything from children.

change your mind

want off the list, or want to know what we have? write to inquiries@thursdaypotato.com.
