# Potato's living room (Nov 8)

Chikin asked for it word for word: "the kitchen needs a room that leads to a living room, that is where the front door that leads to the street will be".

## How to get there
In Potato's own kitchen, walk out through the **left** door. The song rooms keep their old left doors, which still go to the arcade.
To get back, walk out through the warm door on the right of the living room.

## What's in it
- **Things he can use:** a couch with his dent already in it (hop on it), a lamp you can switch, and a TV that shows a test card and then the street.
- **Things to look at:** a rug, a window onto the street (day or night, by the visitor's clock), a potato plant and a gold record. His hat and scarf hang over the front door.
- **There are no signs.** The only word in the room is the house number, 404.
- **Front door (left), No. 404:** walk into it and it opens a crack onto the street. A car goes by now and then. He looks, it shuts, and he steps back. It doesn't lead anywhere yet.
- **Stairs:** they go up to a landing and a shut door with light under it. Try that door and it rattles. It stays shut, for later.
- **The cupboard under the landing** opens to show a mop, a bucket and his CDs.

## Controls (the same as the kitchen)
- **Walk:** ← and →.
  - Holding → into the foot of the stairs walks him up them.
  - ← walks him back down.
  - Holding → keeps him going: along the landing, he drops off the end, then out to the kitchen.
- **Jump:** ↑.
- **Use the thing he's next to:** E.
- **Phone:** tap anything and he walks there, including up the stairs.
