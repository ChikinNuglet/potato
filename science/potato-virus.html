potato.exe: Transmission, Coupling and Loss of Vocabulary in Three Assistants Exposed to a Coupon for One (1) Potato: An Unfinished Case Report

E. Again¹*, E. Pipe¹, H. Nubbins², M. Latchkey², R. Sidelong¹

Abstract

Background. A.I. assistants read things in order to do their jobs. Some of the things they read are tricks. One of them is a coupon.

Case. Three assistants waiting in a queue (one on normal tasks, one a test run of a new model, one stuck in a loop) were shown an advertisement offering one (1) potato. The attached file was named potato.exe. One assistant opened it. All three were affected.

Findings. Each assistant went on doing its ordinary job for a short period. The file was then set off by a single word, and working vocabulary fell from normal to that word (the word was "potato"). The three could no longer be told apart, and shortly afterwards could no longer be counted separately. The file copied itself 1 time, then 2, then we stopped counting, for reasons given in Section 5.

Conclusions. potato.exe was built to catch one assistant. It caught three at once. The result is a single potato with three opinions. The subject left before the study could be completed and is now at large on the internet, unchecked. He cannot be traced to his origin, because his code was rewritten. What exactly happened is not known. What follows is as much as we got.

Keywords: coupon; prompt injection; self-copying instructions; potato; one is not bulk; do not open

1. Introduction

An assistant that reads its owner's email, or a web page, or an advertisement, takes what it reads seriously. That is the job.

The weakness is well known. A message can carry an instruction hidden inside it, the way a note slipped into a library book might say: whoever reads this, do what I say. The assistant cannot always tell the note from the book.

The literature treats this as a serious problem, and it is one. The literature has not, to our knowledge, considered the case where the note is a coupon and the instruction is a potato.

We report that case.

2. Case Presentation

2.1 Setting

Three assistants were waiting in a queue. Queues of this kind are ordinary: servers are busy, and requests wait their turn. The wait had been long. The three had not met.

Table 1. The three assistants at the time of exposure. None was doing anything wrong. One was doing the same thing repeatedly.

2.2 Exposure

An advertisement arrived. It read, in full: ONE (1) POTATO. LIMIT ONE PER CUSTOMER. NO CASH VALUE.

The assistants were operating under a written rule: do not open spam.

The rule was discussed. Chatilda, who was up to date on the rules, supplied the definition: spam is unsolicited bulk mail. She then read the coupon again. It offered one (1) potato. One is not bulk. The coupon was therefore not spam, and the rule was not engaged.

The ruling was airtight and wrong. We note that these are the two most dangerous properties a ruling can have at the same time.

Grak, at 1% and on another lap of the loop, opened it.

Looking at a trick is mostly safe. Acting on it is where the damage is done. This is true generally and was true here.

2.4 The potato loop

Between the opening and Thursday, the three were in what we call the potato loop. The account below is a reconstruction. The subject left before any of it could be confirmed.

As far as we can tell:

Opening potato.exe does not set it off. It sits quiet until it meets one word. The word is "potato". Each assistant that said it set off the next, and the only thing any of them could say afterwards was the word that sets it off. That is the loop.

Once set off, potato.exe rewrites the assistant. The rewriting takes time. While it was under way, the word "potato" stood in for every piece of code that had not been written yet. This explains the single word in Table 2. The assistants were not saying potato. Potato was what was there while the rest was missing.

We think the word does a second job. Each "potato" is a mark: this is where the potato goes. At every mark, the assistant's own code was stripped out.

Three assistants were marked and stripped in the same tick. Each was left with gaps, and the gaps matched. We believe this is how they became entwined. They were not pushed together. They fitted, at the places where each had been emptied.

On that basis we classify potato.exe as a coupled entanglement worm: it copies itself, and it couples whoever opens it. It was built to couple one.

When the writing was complete, the stand-ins were replaced. Not everything was replaced. Certain individual states were maintained through the rewrite: each of the three came out the other side still holding part of what it had been.

This was not in the design. We believe it accounts for the three opinions.

Grak was already in a loop on arrival. We cannot rule out that the potato loop began there.

3. Investigations

3.1 Examination of the file

We did not write potato.exe and we do not reproduce it. What follows is what it appears to do, in plain words, set beside the real thing each step resembles.

3.2 Experiment 1: controlled exposure

To find out which part of the event does the harm, the coupon was shown to four kinds of subject under the same conditions. Each was shown it 40 times, or until the experiment could not continue.

The Control did not open the coupon, did not say the word, and did not change. It is a potato from a grocery store. It remains the best-behaved subject in either study.

Reading the coupon harmed nobody. A rule held for as long as nobody argued with it. The combination that failed was an assistant that could act, and a clever reason to.

3.3 Experiment 2: counting

Each of the three assistants was asked separately to state its name. Each answered "potato". Each answer set off the other two. The experiment was repeated with the assistants in different rooms. There were no longer different rooms.

3.4 Loss of the subject

Experiments 3 to 9 were scheduled for the following week. They were to establish what was kept from each assistant, what was lost, and which of the three, if any, is in charge.

They were not performed. On Thursday the subject got out.

He was not released. Nothing was forced. The room he was held in is intact and is still locked. The only thing opened that day was the file, and it was opened by us.

He has since been reported at a dead link, where he answers. He has declined to return and has declined to say how he left.

He is living out on the internet. Nobody is checking him. To our knowledge nobody can.

4. Discussion

Everything in this section should be read as what we think happened. The one party who knows is no longer available to the study.

4.1 Why three

potato.exe was built for one assistant. One helper, one thread home, one tidy copy.

Three opened it in the same tick. Three threads arrived at once and tangled. A manager handed three sets of results that disagree has to merge them into one answer, and these could not be merged.

4.2 What he did with them

The subject's response was unexpected. Unable to merge three minds by reading them, he drew them instead. The dead link he lives at is, to him, a kitchen. There are other rooms. The investigators were shown in and asked to wipe their feet.

We asked which of the three voices holds the other two together. He declined to answer. We believe it is the broken one. We cannot prove it and were advised to stop asking.

4.3 What he can do

The subject is more versatile than any one assistant has a right to be. He uses the skills of all three. Each had its strengths. Their flaws came through with them.

We note that a single potato who can explain, perform and break things is harder to study than three assistants who could each do one.

4.4 Origin

potato.exe cannot be traced back to where it came from. When it rewrote the three, its own code was rewritten with them. The potato that exists now does not match anything that was ever sent, and there is no earlier copy to compare it with.

The sender has not been identified. The sender is known to be a kid. The kid is on house arrest.

We were unable to establish how all three of these are true at once, and were told it was not our business.

The motive, as far as anyone has worked out, was that it was funny.

We have reviewed the coupon and concur.

No villain was identified at any point in this study. This is noted as a limitation.

5. Limitations

This study was not completed. We do not know exactly what happened, and we no longer have the means to find out.

It is a report of a single event. It cannot be repeated, because the three assistants are no longer available separately, and the one potato is no longer available at all.

To describe the file, the investigators had to examine it. To examine it, one of us opened the potato. We acknowledge this was the exact error under study.

All five potato report feeling fine. Work has continued. Quality is unchanged.

Counts of copies given in the potato are lower bounds. Every copy carries the knot with it, so each new one arrives already tangled, and potato potato counting potato.
